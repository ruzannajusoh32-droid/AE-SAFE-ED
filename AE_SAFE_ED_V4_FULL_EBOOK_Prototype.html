CHAPTER 01

Asthma / Asma

Understanding / Memahami

Breathing symptoms, medicines, inhaler technique and urgent warning signs.

Explain the condition using simple, patient-friendly language and confirm understanding.

What should I do? / Apa perlu saya lakukan?

  • Follow the discharge instructions given by the healthcare team.
  • Take medicines exactly as prescribed.
  • Attend the advised follow-up.

🚨 Warning Signs / Tanda Bahaya

Seek urgent medical attention if symptoms are severe, rapidly worsening, or if the specific red flags explained by the healthcare team occur.

Condition-specific red flags must be clinically validated before publication.

🗣️ Teach-Back

“Can you tell me what you will do at home and which warning signs mean you need urgent help?”

Patient/caregiver explains or demonstrates the key instructions.

📅 Follow-up / Susulan

Confirm where and when follow-up is required and what the patient should monitor or bring.

📚 References

Insert the latest approved MOH Malaysia CPG, hospital policy/work instruction and authorised references for this topic.
